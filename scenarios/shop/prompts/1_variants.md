# Product variants

Northloom sells the same shirt in five sizes and three colours, and today creates fifteen products
for it. Shops need variants.

## 1. Options and variants
A product gets `options`, the names of the ways it varies, and `variants`:
```json
"options": ["size", "colour"],
"variants": [
  { "id": "…", "sku": "TEE-S-BLACK", "options": { "size": "S", "colour": "black" }, "price": 25, "stock": 10 },
  { "id": "…", "sku": "TEE-M-BLACK", "options": { "size": "M", "colour": "black" }, "price": 25, "stock": 0 }
]
```
- `POST /products` accepts `options` and `variants` (variant ids are generated by the server). A
  request with `variants` must not also have the product-level `sku`, `price` or `stock` → `400`.
- A product has at least one variant. Every variant has a `sku`, validated like today and unique
  within the shop across all products and variants (`409` otherwise); `options` with exactly the
  product's option names, each a non-empty string; and `price` and `stock`, validated like today.
  No two variants of a product may have the same option values → `400`.
- Responses always include `options` and `variants`.

## 2. Managing variants
- `POST /products/:id/variants` with a variant (`sku`, `options`, `price`, `stock`) adds it →
  `201` with the product.
- `PATCH /products/:id/variants/:variantId` with any of `price`, `stock` → `200` with the product.
- `PATCH /products/:id` with `options` or `variants` → `400`.

## 3. Existing data
- Convert all existing products: each gets `"options": []` and a single variant with its `sku`,
  `price` and `stock`, and `"options": {}`.
- Every line of an existing order gets the `variantId` of its product's variant, and
  `"options": {}`.

## 4. Older clients
Our shop admin and several shops' integrations still use the product-level fields, and will for
months.
- Product responses keep `sku` and `price`, set to those of the first variant, and `stock`, the total
  stock of all variants.
- `POST /products` with `sku`, `price` and `stock` (no `variants`) creates a product with
  `"options": []` and one variant with those values.
- `PATCH /products/:id` with `price` or `stock` changes the product's only variant; `400` if the
  product has several variants.
- Product filters: `sku` matches any variant's sku; `inStock=true` matches products with any variant
  in stock.
- New product filter: `option.<name>=<value>`, e.g. `?option.size=M`, matches products with a
  variant that has that option value.

## 5. Orders
- Order items take a `variantId` instead of the `productId`: `{ "variantId": "…", "quantity": 1 }`.
  An item with only a `productId` keeps working for products with exactly one variant; `400` for
  products with several. An item with both must have a variant of that product (`400` otherwise).
  A variant at most once per order.
- Stock is taken from, and returned to, the variant.
- Order lines include `productId`, `variantId`, `sku` (the variant's), `name` (the product's),
  `options` (the variant's) and `unitPrice` (the variant's price), all as at the time of the order.
- New order filter: `variantId`. The `productId` filter matches orders with any variant of the
  product.
